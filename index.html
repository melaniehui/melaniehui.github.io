About

I'm studying Economics and Psychology at UCLA, which has played a huge role in how I understand the way people interact with the world around them. My studies in psychology help me understand the reasoning behind people's decisions and preferences, while my economics background has taught me to think strategically about turning those insights into tangible outcomes. Both perspectives have shaped how I approach business, especially when it comes to understanding people and finding meaningful ways to connect with them.

I've also been involved in visual arts for over a decade, including formal training, and art has always been a big part of how I approach things. Through art, I've learned how to translate my creative ideas into reality and appreciate the impact that visual expression can have on people. I believe creativity plays an important role in business because connecting with people goes beyond simply understanding what they want. It's also about how you communicate an idea, make someone feel, and create something they'll remember.
